Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy describes how Myths on Clay Tools (the "Application"), an internal tool operated by Matías Osuna for the YouTube channel Myths on Clay, handles information.

Who uses the Application

The Application is used exclusively by the owner of the Myths on Clay YouTube channel to manage that channel's own content. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The Application uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access and why

  • The OAuth access to the owner's own YouTube channel, used only to upload the channel's videos, set thumbnails, upload subtitle tracks, manage playlists and read basic information about the channel's own videos (titles, IDs, status).
  • We do not access, collect or store data about viewers, other channels or other YouTube users.

How data is stored and shared

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. No analytics, advertising or tracking is performed by the Application.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the Application is in use. The owner can revoke access at any time from the Google security settings page, which immediately stops all access; locally stored tokens are then deleted.
