Myths on Clay is an educational YouTube channel that retells the world's mythologies in order, episode by episode, animated in the style of each culture's own art: Greek black-figure vases, carved Viking runestones and Egyptian tomb paintings.

Every episode is written from the classical sources (Hesiod, the Eddas, the Pyramid Texts and others), narrated by Matías, and animated with our own code-based animation tools.
